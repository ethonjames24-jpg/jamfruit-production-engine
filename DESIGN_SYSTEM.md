# DESIGN_SYSTEM.md — JamFruit Creative Production System

**Scope:** creative/visual production rules for JamFruit Stories.  
**Authority:** approved Drive canon and Gate D records.  
**Purpose:** give engineers and generation agents a concise, reproducible canon surface without replacing the full design bible or source assets.

## 1. Core design principle

JamFruit characters are **fruit-first anthropomorphic characters**, not humans wearing fruit costumes.

Every generation must preserve:
- recognizable fruit anatomy;
- repeatable silhouette;
- stable proportions;
- stable palette;
- readable facial acting;
- character-specific wardrobe logic;
- low ambiguity for image-to-video generation;
- original design language.

Do not imitate a protected studio/franchise style.

## 2. Framing and production language

Default pilot production:
- **9:16 vertical**
- mobile-first composition
- clean depth lanes for dialogue and movement
- readable foreground/midground/background relationships
- avoid critical action outside vertical-safe framing
- maintain wardrobe/background value contrast
- exact canon references outrank textual restatement

Regenerated cast approximations cannot establish or replace canon.

## 3. Final-canon pilot cast

### Julie Mango

Production read:
- warm, expressive mango heroine
- fruit-integrated body
- bright tropical/floral wardrobe language
- gold hoop earrings are part of Julie's default-look signature
- readable social/emotional acting
- must remain visually distinct from East Indian Mango

Gate D continuity:
- warm/floral styling
- hoop signature
- confident readable silhouette in crowded market compositions

### East Indian Mango

Production read:
- taller and more tapered than Julie
- fruit-first construction
- olive / cream / gold styling
- more elevated, composed status/temperament signal
- asymmetric crown treatment
- non-hoop jewelry differentiation

Do not collapse East Indian into a recolored Julie.

Gate D continuity:
- taller tapered silhouette
- olive/cream/gold palette
- asymmetric crown
- distinct jewelry/accessory language

### Ackee

Canon gender presentation: **male**.

Production read:
- opened-pod/ackee anatomy must read clearly
- mature, grounded authority
- structured/practical styling
- dry or mildly tired intervention reads better than default anger
- stable enough for community/authority scenes

Gate D continuity:
- opened-pod authority read
- restrained intervention energy

### Guinep

Canon gender presentation: **female**.

This character has an explicit corrected canon rule.

Required:
- fruit-integrated **guinep-green** face/body surface
- bright smooth yellow-green fruit-family treatment
- compact adult-coded proportions
- kinetic/social acting language
- casual mobile wardrobe
- fixed guinep cluster identity
- exactly controlled secondary-guinep construction when the canon reference requires it

Prohibited:
- orange or skin-tone human-style face/body plane
- grape-bunch drift
- fixed gold-hoop signature
- using superseded Guinep exploratory imagery as literal generation authority

Gate D final direction uses the corrected Guinep literal reference and is the production precedent.

### Soursop

Canon gender presentation: **male**.

Production read:
- broad, grounded silhouette
- darker green surface
- restrained practical wardrobe/neutrals
- quiet, observant, underestimated energy
- harder/decisive side should emerge through controlled acting, not default aggression
- spike density/length must remain repeatable and not visually noisy

Gate D continuity:
- broad dark-green read
- restrained practical presentation
- side-eye/deadpan reactions may carry humor without caricature

## 4. Cast differentiation

Hard requirement: each principal character must be identifiable from silhouette, palette and construction without relying solely on face or dialogue.

Key contrasts:
- Julie vs East Indian: warm/floral/hoops vs taller tapered olive/cream/gold/asymmetric-crown/non-hoop
- Guinep vs Soursop: compact bright kinetic/social vs broad dark-green restrained/grounded
- Ackee: unique opened-pod authority shape

Do not flatten the cast into one generic humanoid body template.

## 5. Final-canon pilot locations

### Fruit Tree District Main Street

Identity:
- everyday commercial/social spine of the district
- **not** a second dense market
- warm sunlit community tone
- walkable street
- modest fruit motifs
- benches, street lighting, vegetation
- Jamaican-inspired road/drain/utility/material/transport detail
- mixed concrete/wood/zinc/shopfront language
- tropical geography/hills where useful
- strong 9:16 establishing and medium-street compositions

Avoid:
- polished generic tropical-resort look
- excessive produce stalls
- regenerated character approximations as location canon evidence

### Coronation Fruit Market

Identity:
- dedicated dense market environment
- social compression, gossip and encounter energy
- strong Jamaican environment specificity
- reusable stall/aisle modules
- supports establishing, two-shot and reaction compositions
- must preserve readable depth for passing characters and eyeline staging

Generated slogans/signage are **placeholder set dressing only** unless separately approved as story copy.

This is the governing EP01 location and current provider-benchmark environment.

### Mango Mansion

Identity:
- private residence
- Jamaican/Caribbean architectural fit
- gated exterior and landscaped grounds
- veranda/balcony language
- foyer, salon, terrace/dining and stair/reveal coverage
- repeatable warm materials, wood/ironwork and tropical planting
- usable 9:16 exterior establishing, interior two-shot and reveal/reaction zones

Avoid generic Mediterranean-resort drift. Preserve lived-in island-home cues.

## 6. EP01 Gate D visual precedent

Episode 1 approved sequence establishes the current production bar:
- S01 market establishing
- S02 Guinep/Soursop whisper two-shot
- S03 three-character Julie reveal
- S04 Guinep guilty-recovery with Soursop counterpoint
- S05 Julie tracking East Indian deeper in aisle
- S06 Julie/East Indian call-and-turn social standoff
- S07 Ackee authority insert
- S08 East Indian owns center and flips the story
- S09 Julie turns on Guinep; Guinep nervous; Soursop restrained side-eye

The purpose of this list is action hierarchy and continuity. It is not authorization to modify the frozen storyboard.

## 7. Performance and animation implications

Generation/animation should preserve:
- who owns the frame;
- eyeline relationships;
- foreground/background hierarchy;
- social distance;
- reaction timing;
- character-specific energy.

A technically attractive frame fails if it changes the intended action hierarchy.

For image-to-video:
- use approved stills/keyframes as the starting authority;
- avoid text-to-video substitution unless a later decision explicitly authorizes it;
- motion should extend the approved composition rather than redesign it.

## 8. Voice-performance compatibility

Visual canon and voice casting are separate gates.

Do not assign a voice based solely on visual stereotype.

Current voice work is evaluating Jamaican authenticity, Patwa pronunciation, emotion, character suitability, accent stability, proper names, repeatability and operational suitability.

Eleven v4 expressive tags such as laughter, sighing, whispers and emotional delivery may improve character acting, but those controls must be tested and approved; they do not change visual canon.

## 9. Asset-control rules

For production comparisons:
- use exact approved Drive asset references;
- track source record and version;
- preserve rejected/superseded assets as history, not authority;
- do not silently replace a reference because a newer generation “looks better”;
- do not treat contact sheets or placeholder signage as permission to invent canon details.

## 10. Change control

The following require explicit approval before implementation:
- character silhouette/anatomy changes;
- signature wardrobe/accessory changes;
- location identity changes;
- changes to EP01 script/storyboard;
- replacement of Gate D approved frames;
- new permanent signage/story-world copy;
- new principal pilot characters or locations.

If a provider cannot reproduce the canon reliably, the provider fails the test. The canon is not automatically weakened to accommodate the provider.
